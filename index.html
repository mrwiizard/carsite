Meet the dealer

A dealership built on a handshake and a fair price

I opened Best Buy Automobile in 2009 with a simple idea: sell the kind of cars I'd put my own family in, and price them so nobody leaves wondering whether they got a good deal.

Today, from our showroom on Strandvägen in Östermalm, most of our customers come to us through referrals. I still check every car personally, test-drive every trade-in myself and make sure each vehicle is right before it gets a price tag.

“If I wouldn't drive it, I won't sell it. That's the promise behind every car on our lot.”
